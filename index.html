backdrop

A 3D robot walking inside flat 2D paintings.

Each map is a single painted image. A camera matched to the painting and invisible 3D geometry give it depth: the robot walks behind objects, climbs stairs and casts shadows.

Move

  • Walk thereClick
  • Run thereDouble-click
  • WalkWASD
  • Runhold Shift

Arrow keys work too. On AZERTY: Z Q S D.

Explore

  • Show the hidden geometryG
  • Sit on the benchClick bench
  • Music on / offM
  • ControlsH

The debug view draws what the painting hides: the boxes that occlude the robot, the walkable area and the exits.
